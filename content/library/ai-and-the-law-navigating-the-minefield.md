Artificial intelligence is no longer the stuff of science fiction – it’s a reality that is rapidly transforming industries and infiltrating our daily lives. As lawyers, we can’t afford to ignore the seismic shift that AI is unleashing on the legal landscape. From liability for autonomous vehicles to ownership of AI-generated IP, the questions raised by this powerful technology are as complex as they are unprecedented. In this post, we’ll dive into the legal minefield of AI, exploring the most pressing challenges across practice areas and offering strategies to help lawyers stay ahead of the curve.

This article is general information about the law, not legal advice about your situation. Reading it does not create an attorney-client relationship with Rothrock Legal; that happens only when both sides sign an engagement letter. The law changes, and how it applies depends on facts we have not seen. Before you rely on anything here, confirm it with a lawyer.
